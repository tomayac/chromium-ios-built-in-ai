Chromium AI privacy policy

Last updated October 20, 2026.

Chromium AI is an experimental build of the open source Chromium browser for iOS that gives web pages on-device AI APIs. It is a personal project of Thomas Steiner.

Data the developer receives

None. The app contains no analytics, no advertising, and no accounts, and it sends nothing to the developer.

AI prompts

Prompts, images, and audio that web pages pass to the AI APIs are processed on the device, by Apple Foundation Models or by the Gemma model stored in the app. They are not sent to any server by the app. A web page that calls the APIs sees its own prompts and answers, and can do with them what its own privacy policy says.

Model download

On devices without Apple Intelligence, the app downloads the Gemma model files from Hugging Face on first use. Hugging Face receives the request, including the device's IP address, as with any download.

Browsing

As with any browser, the websites you visit, and the search engine you choose, receive the requests the app makes to them. Browsing data, such as history and cookies, stays on the device until you delete it in the app's settings. The AI models stay until you delete the app.

TestFlight

If you test the app through TestFlight, Apple shares crash reports and the feedback you choose to send with the developer, as described in Apple's TestFlight privacy notice.

Contact

Questions go to the project's issue tracker.
